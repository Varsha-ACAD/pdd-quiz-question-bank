The Corner Kitchen PRDRead first

1. Problem Statement & Description

The Corner Kitchen, a restaurant with a fixed menu, needs a simple command-line application that lets a customer see the menu, add items to a cart, delete items, and place the order. Each menu item has an item ID, a name and a price, and the amount to be paid is the total value of the items in the cart based on each item's price and quantity.

2. Interface

The application is a command-line program. Every time it waits for a choice, it first shows the menu and the cart, followed by the list of actions.

==================================================
 THE CORNER KITCHEN -- MENU
==================================================
 101  Classic Burger        Rs 180
 102  Paneer Wrap           Rs 160
 103  French Fries          Rs 120
 104  Cold Coffee           Rs  90
 105  Chocolate Brownie     Rs 150
==================================================
YOUR CART
--------------------------------------------------
 (empty)

Amount to pay: Rs 0

2. Delete item

4. Exit

Once items are added, the cart shows them:

Amount to pay: Rs 510

3. Functional Requirements

FR 1: Show the menu and the cart, then the list of actions

  • The menu and the current cart are shown every time before the customer chooses an action.
  • The cart lists each item by name, with its quantity and amount, in the order the items were added.
  • The total amount to pay is shown below the cart.
  • An empty cart is shown as empty, with an amount to pay of Rs 0.

FR 2: Add an item to the cart

  • The customer gives the item ID and the quantity required.
  • If the item is not already in the cart, it is added with that quantity.
  • If the item is already in the cart, the new quantity is added to the quantity already there.
  • Every change is acknowledged with a message showing the running total for that item.
Enter item ID: 101
Enter quantity: 2
Classic Burger added to your cart.

Enter item ID: 101
Enter quantity: 3
Classic Burger quantity is now 5.

FR 3: Delete an item from the cart

  • The customer gives the item ID of an item in the cart, and it is deleted.
  • Deleting an item removes it entirely — all of its quantity at once, not one at a time.
  • Deleting an item updates the amount to pay.
Enter item ID: 104
Cold Coffee deleted from your cart.

FR 4: Place the order

  • An order can be placed only if the cart has at least one item.
  • On placing, the application shows the final cart and the amount to pay, confirms the order, and then ends.
Order placed successfully.
 Classic Burger    x2   Rs 360
 French Fries      x1   Rs 120
Amount to pay: Rs 480

4. Edge Cases

In each case the application prints the stated message and lets the customer continue ordering.

#SituationExpected behaviour
EC 1The item ID is not a number (e.g. abc)Print Invalid item ID.; the cart is unchanged.
EC 2The item ID does not exist on the menu (e.g. 199)Print No such item on the menu.; the cart is unchanged.
EC 3The quantity is not a whole number, or is negative (e.g. -2, two)Print Invalid quantity.; the cart is unchanged.
EC 4The customer tries to delete an item that is not in the cartPrint That item is not in your cart.; the cart is unchanged.
EC 5The customer tries to place an order with an empty cartPrint Your cart is empty.; the order is not placed and the customer may continue.
